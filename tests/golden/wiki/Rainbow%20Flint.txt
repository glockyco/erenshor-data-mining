'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[Quest Items|Quest Item]] in [[Erenshor]].

=Location=
{{PAGENAME}} is found in the study as part of the quest {{QuestLink|link=The Mystery of Bellwain Island}}. It is in a small safe against the wall of the room.

=Description=
{{PAGENAME}} is used to light the torches surrounding the statue on the island. Clicking a torch multiple times changes its color:

<span style="color: red">Red</span> → <span style="color: blue">Blue</span> → White → <span style="color: orange">Orange</span> → <span style="color: green">Green</span> → <span style="color: red">Red</span>

Once all the torches are lit the correct colors, the statue in the middle will rise.

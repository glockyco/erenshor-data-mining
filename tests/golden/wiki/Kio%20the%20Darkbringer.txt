Kio the Darkbringer will only appear when you change the lighthouse flame from red to blue using the {{ItemLink|Blue Burnstone}}.

Kio the Darkbringer has a unique mechanic when the blue light from the lighthouse touches him he begins to heal. During the fight, he will heal 3 times with the light from the lighthouse, then the flames burn out and he can no longer use it to heal.

''{{Main|Enemies}}''
=Location=
{{PAGENAME}} can be found in [[The Bone Pits]]. After entering, go straight ahead into the ruins and turn left. {{PAGENAME}} will be in the center of the garden-like room.

=Appearance=
{{PAGENAME}} is a necromancer with a long grey beard. He wears green robes.

=Behavior=
{{PAGENAME}}s waits in the middle of the ruins, and will attack players if they get too close.

{{PAGENAME}} is very similar to [[Woe]] in the [[Island Tomb]] on [[Stowaway's Step]]. A [[Paladin]] or [[Duelist]] can interrupt {{PAGENAME}} while he is casting with a well-timed {{AbilityLink|Kick}}.

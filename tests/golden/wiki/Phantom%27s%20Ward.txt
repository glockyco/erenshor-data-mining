There are two {{PAGENAME}} in this dungeon.

Every once awhile (roughly every 10 second), they become impervious to damage and spawn 4 [[Undying Light]] orbs. One needs to be slain for your party to be able to damage the ward again. The correct Undying Light is the one with the red bobble effect.

The wards respawn if you die and your party flees out the dungeon, or if your party wipes.

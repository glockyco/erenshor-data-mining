'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[:Category:Notes|Note]] in [[Erenshor]].

=Location=
{{PAGENAME}} is a note found on [[Bellwain Island]] as part of the quest {{QuestLink|The Mystery of Bellwain Island}}.

=Description=
{{PAGENAME}} is a poem with clues on what color to light the torches surrounding the statue.

{{ItemTooltip|stablekey=item:quest - bellwain afternoon}}

This note is less direct than others in the quest and requires some extrapolation: the torch at 2 o'clock should be lit white, corresponding with the "snow-like seedlings." As more come next, 3 o'clock should also be white. Finally, the 4'clock torch should be lit blue, hinted at by the color of the sea.

'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[:Category:Notes|Note]] in [[Erenshor]].

=Location=
This note can be found on [[Bellwain Island]] as part of the quest {{QuestLink|The Mystery of Bellwain Island}}.

=Description=
{{PAGENAME}} contains the lyrics for a song that seemingly makes plants grow.

{{ItemTooltip|stablekey=item:quest - bellwain garden song}}

Each of the bells near the garden has a word printed on it. Using the lyrics in the song, click the bells to ring them in the correct order:
*Vision
*Passion
*Luck
*Flourish
*Passion
*Vision
*Luck
The order of the bells (with their names visible) from left to right, is: 2 → 3 → 4 → 1 → 3 → 2 → 4.

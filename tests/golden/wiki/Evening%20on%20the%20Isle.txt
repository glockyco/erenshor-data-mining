'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[:Category:Notes|Note]] in [[Erenshor]].

=Location=
{{PAGENAME}} is a note found on [[Bellwain Island]] as part of the quest {{QuestLink|The Mystery of Bellwain Island}}.

=Description=
{{PAGENAME}} is a poem with clues on what color to light the torches surrounding the statue.

{{ItemTooltip|stablekey=item:quest - bellwain evening}}

This note instructs players to light the torch at 5 o'clock orange. Because it also hints at 6 o'clock being lit with the "same warmth and desire," it should also be lit orange as well.

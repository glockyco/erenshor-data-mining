Located in the rocky northern mountains which is reached by a gigantic slope in the central area of the [[Braxonian Desert]].

To Kill Evadne you need the [[Tempestalia, Ring of Storms]] from the quest chain {{QuestLink|link=Orion Mycon's Lost Ring}}. The effect from the ring allows you to put out the brazier making her vulnerable.

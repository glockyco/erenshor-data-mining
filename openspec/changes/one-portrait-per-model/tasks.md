# Tasks

## Publication permission

This change plans implementation and publication readiness. Do not launch the game, capture models, implement code, or publish during proposal work. During implementation, do not write live files, redirects, descriptions, articles, or generated data without explicit approval of the reviewed publication plan. Capture approval and title wording approval do not grant publication permission. Map deployment needs its own authorization.

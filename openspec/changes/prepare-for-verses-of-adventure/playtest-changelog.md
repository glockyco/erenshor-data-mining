# Erenshor 0.8 playtest changelog

## 1. Dated changes by topic

Source: [developer-post export](../../../output/0.8/discord-playtest-build-brian.md), checked against the [long posts](../../../output/0.8/dev-long.md) and [full channel](../../../output/0.8/discord-playtest-build.md) for reply context. The channel export starts on Oct 9. The first developer post is Oct 13 and the last is Nov 19. Dates below use the export's UTC timestamps. Some posts quote testers verbatim. Those excerpts are not treated as developer confirmations unless he follows up. Patch announcements are evidence of what Brian reported for that build, not proof that it works or that the behavior survived to release.

Comparison baseline: [script review](script-changes.md) and [data review](data-changes.md), both for playtest build 26769622 against 0.7. **Gap** means neither review records the detail. **Partial** means the reviews cover part of the entry but not the detail called out. **Differs** means the post and snapshot describe different behavior, values or names. A dated playtest post can be accurate for its own build and still differ. Section 2 reconciles the material differences.

### Bard: songs, notes and chords

- Oct 13 [Gap]: Brian described Bard as support and crowd control with small group heals, not big single-target heals. Personal DPS is its weakest role. An individual composition can be tuned toward the job it needs to do.
- Oct 15 [Differs]: The first build introduced Bard, composable songs, Hum, more than 50 spells, and three planar spells. The announcement's song list was informal and called the top song Elrehon's Crescendo. The snapshot has seven active songs plus Hum and calls the level 35 song Elrehon's Cacophony.
- Oct 16 [Partial]: Chord: Soothing, a redundant area heal after the other heals became group heals, became Chord: Enthusiasm with 3% haste and 10 mana regeneration at this stage. Comfort Song was fixed. Brian said buffs should last 18 seconds and mezzes last longer. He pointed to the Azure vendor for a mez he thought was around level 29. The reviews list the resulting content, not that patch history. Enthusiasm changed again on Oct 20.
- Oct 16 [Partial]: Invigorate took priority over Enthusiasm because the higher-level buff won within the same line. Brian proposed splitting mana regeneration out. He also explained why mixed-cost notes and chords matter: song cost starts at the average mana cost of its components and a chord cast on resonance is free. The cost and free cast are in the script review, but this specific haste interaction is not.
- Oct 17 to 18 [Partial]: Bard has tools for solo and swarm kiting, including a movement slow. Early songs could bypass resists and target-level restrictions, including an over-level mez. Brian wanted to preserve kiting without making enemies ten levels higher routine. The script review covers the later resist path, not this failure or the design distinction.
- Oct 19 to 20 [Partial]: Effective Dexterity, calculated with Finesse, started reducing song mana cost. The Oct 20 patch set a zero floor. Enthusiasm became haste only, the new Chord: Rapid Thoughts gave 10 mana regeneration, and Chord: Meditative Minor moved to level 12 and a 25 mana bonus. Channeling Minor (level 17, 10%) and Major (level 34, 20%) boosted cast spell damage for the party, not procs. The reviews cover the general formulas and spell names, not this sequence or the stated spell values.
- Oct 20 to 21 [Partial]: Brian recommended putting modifier chords last and confirmed haste and songs affect one raid group, not the whole raid. The Oct 21 patch stopped the spell-damage buff line from overwriting resonance. The source review covers party targeting and the Sim modifier order, not the overwrite fix.
- Oct 25 [Gap]: Brian suggested one damage spell followed by four dissonant chords as a damage composition.
- Oct 31 [Partial]: Brian explained the older crowd-control progression and announced an extended area mez line. He described an unrestricted single-target option, Note: Lullaby Fourth from Willowwatch, and proposed area mez breakpoints of Bard level 4 for targets through 10, level 10 through 21, level 21 through 33 and level 31 through 37. A single-target mez fills tier gaps and is needed above the highest area cap. He floated shorter duration just above a cap, not a shipped rule. The reviews establish the note identity and coded max-level handling, not those exact live progression values.
- Nov 2 [Gap]: No additional song forms were planned for 0.8. The three-slot harmony form is meant to enhance a selected effect rather than every buff. Earlier Hum prototypes played two songs and ascended to five, but Brian backed away because that automated too much of the class.
- Nov 3 [Partial, differs in phrasing]: The patch enabled song resist and level checks and addressed crowd-control and status-effect bugs. Brian described song resists as working like spell resists. The two helpers are similar but not interchangeable in the snapshot.
- Oct 19, Nov 12 and Nov 16 [Partial]: Brian announced professionally recorded replacements for his placeholder guitar sounds. New chord audio was in the Nov 12 patch. He listed final sounds for both chords and notes on Nov 16. The reviews mention audio changes, not what arrived when.

### Bard: charisma, harmony, dissonance, aura, ascensions and gear

- Oct 15, 22 and Nov 2 [Partial, differs in scope]: The early notes described stronger charisma scaling for damage and damage over time, and renamed the displayed proficiency from Mind to Persuasion. On Oct 22 Brian said the Persuasion formula did not handle lower Charisma well. On Nov 2 he said Bard's damage contribution from Charisma was still too small and he planned to increase it that week. The snapshot has a Bard-specific curve but no ordinary spell charisma formula change. Do not take the early wording as a global damage rule.
- Oct 18 [Partial]: Brian said early song damage scaled too hard with Intelligence relative to Charisma. His intended division was Charisma for song damage and buff strength, Intelligence for mana pool and individually cast chords and notes, Dexterity for song timing and crits, and no special song role for Wisdom. He confirmed related proficiencies apply. Charisma also benefits allies through Bard aura. Nov 2 clarified that charisma can raise stats or haste from a buff but does not extend duration. The relevant ascension can extend duration, and the base buff duration was raised from 9 to 18 seconds during development. Nov 5 reiterated the Intelligence penalty outside songs and that Dexterity and Charisma were both meant to be valuable.
- Oct 15 [Differs]: Fernalla planar armor and spells, the Astra Bard weapon, and charisma added to many existing items are documented in the snapshot. The early list also called something a Bard chessboard skill. The snapshot instead adds Minstrel of Sivakaya as a seventh possible Faceless chessboard encounter and has no new skill or item tied to that branch.
- Oct 16 to 18 [Partial]: Brian pointed to a lute vendor in Hidden Hills and to the Old Bonepits Songbook quest as the source of a better lute. The Oct 17 hotfix put Melisma, The First Muse into Astra's loot pool after it was initially missing. Brian gave its damage and delay as 21 and 1.2 while its tooltip was broken. The Oct 18 patch restored its damage display and made the Short Sword of Viropha and Whipsword Bard-usable. The data review gives Astra's final source and general class eligibility changes but not these individual fixes. The Oct 17 testing workaround was not an acquisition route.
- Oct 20 [Gap]: Brian said resonance then gave back some mana and considered removing that return after mana became effectively unlimited. Keep that separate from the later source-confirmed free cast, the Inspiration ticks and any instrument or spell mana effect. Neither review establishes an independent current mana refund on the resonance trigger.
- Nov 3 [Partial]: The Skald patch added two raid weapons and two raid lutes for Bard. These replace the Oct 17 state of no Bard raid weapons. Stable item identities are in the data review.
- Nov 5 [Gap]: Brian described a harmony build as able to add a few points to the haste cap, modestly increase the usable weapon-damage bonus, or make Bard a group healer. He favored the major harmonic modifier for healing and warned the numbers were still in flux. The script review gives the actual effects and aggregation, not a fixed cap increase for a generic harmony build.
- Nov 17 [Partial]: Brian traced an aura losing its bonus on zoning to save/restore retaining the effect but not the caster. New refreshes merely extended an already restored base effect. He reported an aura fix and considered dropping all Bard song buffs across zones to let them rebuild. He warned that gear upgrades might not immediately update an already equipped aura and that charisma-boosted buffs such as haste might have the same transfer problem. The script review documents source-aware auras and the companion transfer exclusion, not all of these cases.
- Nov 17 to 19 [Partial, differs between builds]: Brian confirmed the Entrainment roll was comparing against ascension rank instead of the computed chance, then reported it fixed on Nov 19. He also noted that the ascension improves a learned ability rather than granting the ability itself. The snapshot records calculation and trigger behavior on critical songs, not the earlier broken comparison. Proc text can be shown by enabling Player Spell Results.
- Nov 19 [Partial]: A test report after the aura fix still found the reverse case, a Sim Bard applying an aura to the player after zoning. Brian confirmed that he had not cleared the carried Bard buffs on the player and said he would fix it next. Do not describe the Nov 17 fix as covering player and Sim sources in both directions.

### Zones and encounters

- Oct 15 to 16 [Partial]: Glowmere Grotto opened as a level 22 to 29 dungeon, with routes from Malaroth Beach and from near the Soluna's Landing teleport pad. The first hotfix corrected the direction players faced on entry. The clean graph's names for those two neighboring zones are Malaroth's Nesting Grounds and Soluna's Landing. The graph includes both routes but not the spoken wall gate inside the dungeon.
- Oct 21 [Partial]: Ripper's Keep moved its town inside the walls, added seven class hubs comparable to Port Azure, relocated the teleport pad and added NPCs. Some text was still provisional. Brian said both secret features were hinted at but not yet usable. The reviews identify the rebuilt zone, vendors, NPCs and changed spell landing, not that intermediate phase or the exact physical pad move.
- Oct 22 to 23 [Partial]: Ripper's Keep gate gained a key quest and a new minimap. Glowmere received a minimap. The Keep also gained a hidden quest with a Reliquary item reward and a separately hidden boss of roughly Blight Wyrm difficulty. Brian said the boss is hidden in the zone rather than triggered by that quest. On Oct 22 he also reported a second patch for a Reliquary loading regression. The Oct 23 patch added flavor NPCs. The data review gives quest and NPC rows but not those minimap and difficulty details.
- Oct 31, Nov 2 and Nov 3 [Partial, differs for despawn]: Announced Oct 31 and shipped Nov 3, The Skald sits behind the arena in Vitheo's Plane of Valor, roughly Opus difficulty and intended as an optional enemy to avoid until ready. Four accompanist roles heal, use an expanding area attack, use a stronger ordinary area attack, or dazzle the raid. Brian said the adds should disappear immediately and acknowledged a flag problem when a tester obtained their drops. The script review confirms boss-alive spawn control and linked aggro but no explicit destruction of existing adds in that controller. The guide cannot promise immediate despawn from this evidence.
- Nov 16 [Gap]: Brian listed Port Azure navigation mesh fixes in the scheduled patch, plus extra Sim group spots in Glowmere Grotto, Vitheo's Rest, Island Tomb and Elderstone. The asset and script reviews do not inventory these placements or navmesh changes.
- Nov 18 to 19 [Partial]: The larger, lower-level Faerie's Brake went live with most major routes in the same general arrangement. Brian targeted levels 1 to 5, warned that saved characters might log in under the terrain and advised Unstuck, and said its minimap would be blank until a new file was made. He said he was patching a loud ambient drone, acknowledged missing collision on the large rock and mushrooms, and estimated about 1 minute 20 seconds to walk from Hidden Hills to Port Azure as a level 1. He wanted a smaller Port Azure zone-line area and was still assessing the new scale. The data review covers specific Brake NPC changes, not the scene revision or minimap state.
- Nov 18 [Partial]: Port Azure Spark Beetles became level 1 and 2 Spark Hatchlings. The data review lists the hatchling identity. The stated reason was to make Port Azure viable as a future start with Faerie's Brake next door. Stowaway's Step and Willowwatch remained the start options in the reviewed build.

- Nov 10 [Covered]: Vaeia Sevust, in Fernalla's Revival Plains, offers Find The Watcher, which points players to the Watcher in Willowwatch Ridge. Giving a Riftsnare Gem to the Flicker of Souls now kills it immediately rather than leaving the kill to the player. The existing reviews identify this as a quest turn-in death, not ordinary combat loot eligibility.
- Nov 16 [Gap]: Brian floated having Lucian return the tutorial note so it could be banked for later characters, and moving Pg. 7 again. A tester had confused the reference to a lost soul with Bellwain after getting the page and the pirate clue from the same boss. The data review records Pirate Musings, Pirate's Ledger and Pg. 7's new source, but neither of Brian's follow-up ideas is confirmed shipped.

This is not the raw damage and delay line Melisma temporarily lost. The triage records tooltip work but not this removal and the reason.
- Oct 20 and 29 [Partial]: Brian said Hum is sold in Willowwatch and Stowaway and described upgrades as separate purchases. A tester called the Ripper and Port Azure inventories identical. The snapshot counts 30 and 31 distinct stock items respectively, so do not merge the shops or repeat that claim as a developer statement.
- Nov 4 [Partial]: Raid item levels became tier markers: 42 for trash, 43 for tier 1, 44 for tier 2, 45 for gods and 46 for Brax. Brian flagged this specifically for modders and data consumers. The data review captures re-keyed items and actual level changes, not that mapping. It notes exceptions such as Orbital Loop, so filenames and level alone are not stable identity or sufficient proof of source. The patch also restored green model sparkles on +1 through +5 gear.
- Nov 4 to 5 [Partial, differs]: Brian said Skald held both weapons temporarily, then moved one to Jinx and moved Jinx's renamed waistband to Skald. He initially referred to the latter as Creeping Cord. The actual item identity in the snapshot is former Creeping Waistband, now Observer's Waistband. The clean table still models a small Jinx chance for it alongside Skald, so do not publish the move as an exclusive source. On Nov 5 Brian said Screeching Lute changed from a 3% incoming damage amplification effect to an activated 2% Amplified Pain effect. The reviews list the instrument, effect identity and generic amplification path but not that balance history. He separately clarified that Painted Lute applies a group buff and said it should not require selecting a target.
- Nov 16 [Partial]: Vendor sorting was slated to be fixed after scrolling changed item order. Brian said spells should be in level order from opening the shop. The script review supplies the type grouping and notes that quest-added stock is appended separately.

### Combat balance and mechanics outside Bard

- Oct 15 [Partial]: The early build fixed Group Regrowth's healing, incorrect dummy totals, duplicate on-screen skill damage for attacks like cleave, kick and backstab, and Reaver aggro stances. The script review covers the dummy timer, extra DPS credit removal and corrected attacker stance handling, not the original dummy undercount or the named heal's correction.
- Oct 20 [Partial, differs in scope]: Brian announced a randomized nerf to the non-proc wand hit, roughly halving it on average, and higher base damage for late Arcanist direct-damage spells. The source applies a fresh 0.1 to 1.0 multiplier to the whole pre-round wand total, not merely the item's base-damage part. It is in shared wand code, not an Arcanist-only branch. The data review finds four continuing spell base increases. Brian later said his raid DPS tests recovered part of the lost damage through Bard buffs, not that a fixed DPS value was guaranteed.
- Oct 23 [Gap]: Brian explained that the Fallen Druids' Thornwhip is an early area attack that fires every seven seconds and cannot be interrupted. He also said leashing mobs are intentionally immune during their return. Oct 18 placed the druids after Krakengard in intended progression and tentatively named Krakengard as another source of rubies. Those are explanations, not evidence of new 0.8 spawn or drop rules.
- Oct 26 [Gap]: The patch stopped group heals from healing twice. The script review describes status and cast resolutions but does not record this fix.
- Nov 18 [Partial, differs in scope]: A patch corrected some monster spells applying damage over time where instant damage was intended. The same patch made the formerly ineffective player Melee Proficiency skill book meaningful. Brian described the benefit as all combat rolls treating the player as one level higher. The reviewed code supports a +1 level-difference adjustment in the melee hit calculation, not a global effective-level increase.

### SimPlayers

- Oct 13 to 16 [Partial]: Brian initially said Sim Bards were nearly ready. The first build let players add 20 more Sims than before, including freshly generated Bards. He advised befriending some while playing a new character so they would not tether to an older high-level character. On Oct 16 he reported passive visual culling for Sims behind walls. The reviews show class and population changes and graphics culling, not the extra cap headroom or this player advice.
- Oct 15 [Partial]: Loadouts let players set build tendencies, save or restore equipment sets and use quick swaps in Sim Inspect or Raid Manager. Sim Reavers got missing innate skills, Windblades recovered some low-level scaling, spell-database fixes let Sims use previously broken spells, and Imbued Arrow's physical scaling was corrected. The script review covers the gearing and skill paths and the bow scaling correction, not all individual class and database fixes.
- Oct 17 [Gap]: In playtest, Brian suggested targeting a Sim and using `/iamadev` with `/my music` to inspect Bard choices, and `/loadset X` to assign a test level and basic gear. These are diagnostic commands, not player-facing guide steps or a way to author a Sim song.
- Oct 18 to 19 [Partial, differs for level filtering]: The Bard tab in Group Tasks sets behavior. Early Sims selected chords below, not at, their own level, while also casting song forms above their level. The patch corrected both cases and enabled raid buffing and melee damage benefits for Sims. It made them spend less time adjusting combat position, maintain buffs over most damage songs against targets more than three levels higher if more than one buff was possible, and retain raid song selections between zones. The script review covers the planner and targeting, but still finds highest-modifier helpers with no level test. Do not generalize the patch claim to every chord-selection path.
- Oct 27 to 29 [Partial]: Stormcallers stopped asking for shields and Sim name tags were fixed after death. Group roles, healing, pulling, DPS and Bard settings gained persistence. The following patch made group members reorderable by dragging their lifebars. The latter two behaviors are in the script review. The shield request and name tag fixes are not.
- Nov 4 [Partial]: Rivals, especially Friends Club, gain access to raid gear from earlier tiers as the player advances and can acquire +1 through +5 gear, rare blessed pieces, and ascended items. The playtest milestone examples were kill Arbor, spawn Honsus, defeat Soluna and defeat Brax. Non-Friends Club Sims can get raid loot too but typically stop around armor sets without help. Brian said retroactive advancement was intended before the patch went live, and Brax loot for rivals would await a later raid tier. The script review shows tier writes, gear rules and cached-tier caveats, not retroactive backfill or a guarantee of instant access.
- Nov 5, 7 and 9 [Partial]: The new progression loop lets eligible Sims level and gear up out of zone during long sessions. Brian observed his test Sims rising from level 3 to 15 in a few hours. Nov 9 constrained gear brought into Stowaway and made Sims less likely to equip pickaxes or Pupil's Pins. The scripts document off-zone eligibility and the low-level tutorial item cap, not the item-specific anti-preferences or a guaranteed catch-up rate.
- Nov 6, 12 and 17 [Partial]: Sims began sheathing weapons out of combat. Auto Gear Swap to Phys was updated to favor haste. The Nov 17 patch announcement also listed improved main-assist behavior. The reviews cover sheathing, worn haste scoring and one raid assist branch, but not the announcements' full scope.
- Nov 13 to 14 [Gap]: Brian investigated guilded Sims accepting invitations outside the level range and then failing when they arrived. He initially suspected off-zone level changes, later considered allowing guildmates to join regardless of level, and said a set of fixes went live. The posts do not resolve the exact final rule for inviting a higher-level guildmate.
- Nov 19 [Gap]: Existing worlds will not be preseeded with high-level Sim Bards for release. Brian recommends inviting low-level Sims and catching them up, including in raids. He mentioned an XP penalty in mixed-level groups and thought it did not apply to raids. His under-an-hour estimate for a level 1 raider to reach 35 is an example, not a universal leveling rate or verified formula.

### Save data and release compatibility

- Oct 15 [Partial]: The new schema added songs, Sim loadouts and gear tendencies, and Bard identifiers. Old saves can move forward into 0.8. A playtest save cannot safely be taken back to older retail. Brian repeated the warning because the current retail build did not understand the new save. The source review covers fields and legacy defaults, not reverse compatibility.
- Oct 19, 26 and 28 [Covered]: Raid song selection persists across zoning. Group settings and the quest reminder gained persistent state. The script review documents per-Sim roles and healing and DPS settings, player pull settings and tracked quest identity. Oct 26 also contains a report of one-touch preferences switching back after logout, but the source review finds save/load fields and the posts do not confirm a separate fix.
- Nov 5 [Covered]: Background Sim progression required new save handling. The source review documents queued level changes, bonus gear time and flush points. Brian asked testers to look for missing gear or spells.
- Nov 9 [Partial]: Bank saves stopped creating unbounded backups and a cleanup script removed older copies. Restoring a backup no longer overwrites current data before confirmation. The script review documents bank backup rotation and cleanup, but describes backup-loader changes as equivalent bookkeeping and does not confirm that change in the confirmation flow.
- Nov 13 to 14 [Partial]: Steam Cloud reliability work was being tested. Brian advised starting a fresh game process before using the server-reset control because resetting after leaving a character for the menu can retain data in memory. He reported an item-deletion bug and later said fixes were live without naming the affected action. The review documents cloud and reset code and bank safety, not this specific item-loss trigger.
- Nov 16 [Partial]: Instead of asking Steam to sync hundreds of granular save files, the game writes one ZIP on shutdown. At startup it restores a ZIP from another device only when its identifier differs from the local identifier. With Steam Cloud disabled, the archive is ignored. Brian confirmed Sim population cap is intentionally device-local, so a PC and Steam Deck can use different caps even when save contents sync. The source review gives archive paths and behavior, not the deliberate local-cap distinction.

## 3. Release watch through Dec 1

Brian said on Nov 13 that the playtest build current on release day would become retail. This is not a promise that build 26769622 is the release build. Re-export the final depot and preserve the distinction between known planned work and suggestions below.

### Announced or open at the cutoff

- Faerie's Brake minimap and geometry (Nov 18 to 19) [Gap]: Brian explicitly said the rebuilt zone still needed a minimap, expected to shrink the Azure zone-line area, and was giving the overall scale a few days. Refresh tiles, spawn and path placements, entrances, and the world-map overlay against the final scene. Check the lower-level mobs, collision fixes and whether old saved positions still need Unstuck.
- Port Azure as a starting location (Nov 18) [Gap]: Brian called it a near-future objective, not a confirmed Dec 1 feature. If enabled, check start selection, starter coordinates and bind point, tutorial handling and guide graph entry. Do not infer the feature from Spark Hatchlings and the Brake revamp alone.
- Bard buffs cast by Sims when the player zones (Nov 19) [Gap]: Brian promised a next-patch fix for the inverse aura-transfer case. ## 4. Dated mechanics notes for wiki and guide

| Post date | Player question | Explanation to carry forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oct 13 and 15 | What does a Bard do? | Support, crowd control and small group healing are the main strengths. Buffs contribute to other players' damage, so personal DPS understates group or raid contribution. The [song book record](data-changes.md#ascensions-and-book) points to A Guide To Songwriting, granted to a new Bard. |
| Oct 16, 20 and 25, Nov 2 | How do I build and play a song? | Slot learned notes and chords in an ordered song form with a fixed capacity. Different forms favor different roles. A player can cast these spells individually or as components, can play a composition with one touch when configured, and can put an eligible friendly component into Hum. The cheap components lower the average mana cost. Dexterity scaled by Finesse reduces cost, with a zero player floor. The developer puts modifier chords at the end and gave one damaging component followed by four dissonant modifiers as a damage example. See [the implementation](script-changes.md#spells-skills-and-songs) for capacities, note timing and the Hum restriction rather than extrapolating from that example. |
| Oct 20 and 25, Nov 2 and 5 | What do harmony and dissonance do? | Dissonance lets a composition emphasize offensive effects. Harmony lets it emphasize friendly effects such as healing or party buffs, including haste. A three-slot harmony form forces prioritization. Gear and modifier chords affect the respective modifiers. The developer's examples are build advice, not fixed multipliers or global cap increases. The script review gives the actual accumulation order. |
| Oct 18 to 20, Nov 2 and 5 | What do Bard attributes change? | Charisma plus Persuasion scales song damage and buff strength rather than normal buff duration. The relevant ascension can extend duration. Intelligence supports mana pool and weaker standalone casting. Dexterity plus Finesse speeds different-note transitions, helps song criticals and reduces song cost. The reviewed source also applies the Charisma multiplier to song direct healing. Use the actual curve and repeated application in the source for numeric examples, not Brian's early playtest descriptions as an exact formula. |
| Oct 16 and 20, Nov 17 and 19 | What do resonance, aura and Entrainment do? | Resonance lets a Bard cast a solo chord without spending mana while the charge lasts. The snapshot has a short-lived charge, with an ascension chance of a second. Aura stat conversion uses the singer's charisma and benefits allies rather than converting the singer's stats onto themselves. Bard songs buff only the singer's group within a raid. Entrainment can trigger an immediate status tick on a critical song. The ascension improves the chance and Player Spell Results shows its proc text. Do not promise aura persistence on zoning until the reverse Sim-to-player case is verified. |
| Oct 31 and Nov 3 | Why did a mez fail? | Early builds wrongly allowed over-level mezzing. The extended area line has tier ceilings, target levels and resist checks, so switch to the single-target option when the area tier cannot cover the enemy. Lullaby Fourth was described as having no target-level limit, which is not immunity to resists. Verify the announced spell levels against release data before tabulating them. |
| Oct 15, 16 and 22 | How do I get to Glowmere Grotto? | Use the entrance on the Malaroth Beach side, listed as Malaroth's Nesting Grounds in the data, or the route near the teleport pad in Soluna's Landing. The wall and keyword are source evidence, not map directions given by Brian in these posts. Neither exterior route is the wall gate. |

In data, the quest also costs 5 Azure Guard faction and adds 1 each to Citizens of Erenshor and Ripper DeMerth.

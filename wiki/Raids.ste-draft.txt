The Planar March update (patch 0.7) added '''raids''' to [[Erenshor]]. A raid is the largest group activity in the game. A usual party has five members. A raid has three parties of five members. This makes 15 members in total: the player and 14 [[Simulated Players]] from the player's [[Guild]].

The game has four raids. Each raid is an Ethereal Plane. A portal in the [[Reliquary Hall]] gives access to each plane. To open a portal, put the correct rune into one of the four outer slots in the Reliquary. Raid enemies are level 40 to 42, and raid bosses are level 43.

All raid members should be maximum level. They should also be equipped with high level gear from pre-raid bosses and dungeons. For gear advice, refer to [[Recommended_Gear#Pre-Raid_Recommended]].

The zone page for each raid gives the full list of its enemies and its bosses.

[[Vitheo's Plane of Valor]] is different from the other three raids. Most of this raid is an arena with eight rounds. For each round, the loot comes from a [[Vithean Chest|Vithean Chest]] and not from the boss.

==Unlock the Raids==

To unlock the four raids, you must make four raid runes. Each raid rune needs one Inert Rune and one {{ItemLink|Eldrich Crystal}}.

Many of the steps below have their own long chain of prerequisites. For the full procedure, refer to '''[[Raid Unlocking Walkthrough]]'''.

===Step 1: Get the four Inert Runes===

The four Inert Runes have four different sources.

* {{ItemLink|Inert Rune of Vitheo}}. Defeat [[Apparition of Vitheo]] in [[Vitheo's Rest]].
* {{ItemLink|Inert Rune of Fernalla}}. Defeat [[Fallen Fernalla]] in [[Blooming Sepulcher]].
* {{ItemLink|Inert Rune of Soluna}}. Defeat [[Astra, Rogue of the Stars]] in [[Soluna's Landing]]. Then give the {{ItemLink|Heart of Astra}} to the [[Freed Spirit of Raval]].
* {{ItemLink|Inert Rune of Brax}}. Defeat the [[Braxonian Planar Guard (Fire)]] and the [[Braxonian Planar Guard (Ice)]] in [[Fallen Braxonia]]. Then give their two portal shards to [[Braxon Elden]] in [[Braxonian Desert]].

===Step 2: Open the Secluded Sanctuary===

The gate to [[Secluded Sanctuary]] is on [[Stowaway's Step]]. To open the gate, complete the quest {{QuestLink|Completing the Ritual}}. Give these four items to [[Lucian Revald]]:

* {{ItemLink|Loss Stone}}
* {{ItemLink|Demented Brain}}
* {{ItemLink|A Remnant of Sivakaya}}
* {{ItemLink|Nightmare Crystal}}

===Step 3: Get four Eldrich Crystals===

Defeat the [[Azynthian Keeper]] in [[Secluded Sanctuary]]. It always drops one {{ItemLink|Eldrich Crystal}}. You need one crystal for each of the four runes.

===Step 4: Make the four raid runes===

Give one Inert Rune and one {{ItemLink|Eldrich Crystal}} to the related energy. Three of these NPCs have the name [[Verdant Energy]]. The fourth has the name [[Elemental Energy]].

==Start a Raid==

To start a raid, push the '''Call Raid''' button in the [[Guild]] window. Three conditions apply.

* '''You must be in the [[Reliquary Hall]].''' In other zones, the game shows this message: "Raids must be formed in a Reliquary." A game option removes this condition.
* '''You must not be in a group.''' First, dismiss all group members. If you do not, the game shows this message: "Dismiss your group before forming a raid."
* '''Members of Erenshor's Young cannot start a raid.''' The game shows this message: "Erenshor's Young cannot participate in raids."

When you start a raid, the game calls your guild to the hall. The game also puts your A-Team [[Simulated Players]] into the raid slots. To add more Sim Players, select a Sim Player and add it to an empty slot.

Only the [[Reliquary Hall]] and the four planes permit a raid. If you go to a different zone, the raid stops. The game then shows this message: "A strange force pulls your companions in different directions... (This is not a raidable zone)."

During a raid, enemies below level 40 give no experience. Thus you cannot use a raid to get experience from lower level content.

==The Raid Window==

The raid window replaces the group window. It has 14 slots for [[Simulated Players]] and one card for the player. The player is always in the first group. These controls are the most important tools for the raid leader:

* '''Group task.''' Set one task for each of the three groups. A group can help the first, the second, or the third group. A group can also attack its own target, or attack all adds.
* '''Main Assist and Main Tank.''' This is one role, not two. Each group has one member with this role. The other members attack the target of this member. Healers heal this member first.
* '''Puller.''' Each group can have one puller.
* '''CC and Heals.''' These are flags. More than one member in a group can have them. An example of crowd control is {{AbilityLink|Coma}}.
* '''Heal assignments.''' For each healer, set a percentage of maximum health for each other member. The healer heals that member when the health of that member goes below the percentage. A value of zero removes the assignment.
* '''DPS throttle.''' Increase the delay between the attacks of one group or of the full raid. At high values, Sim Players do not use their spells and skills.
* '''Loot dist.''' Give an item to a raid member. Use this control for {{ItemLink|Sivakrux|text=Sivakruxes}} and {{ItemLink|Planar Stone|text=Planar Stones}}.

All raid members receive the experience from a kill. Each kill also increases the opinion of your Sim Players about you.

==Prepare for a Raid==

Two other pages give more data about raid preparation:

* '''[[Class Roles in the Planar March Raid]]''' shows what each class and each build gives to the raid. It also shows which debuffs are cumulative, and how many tanks, healers, and damage dealers to use.
* '''[[Distributing Gear in the Planar March Raid]]''' shows the priority of the stats and the correct weapon for each build. It also shows which auras to use, and which gear to upgrade first.

This is a short summary. These are recommendations from the two guides, not strict requirements:

* '''Tanks.''' Collect Agility and AC.
* '''Resistances.''' Increase the lowest resistance of each member. The guide recommends 140% before the late encounters, and 170% to 190% at the end.
* '''Damage dealers.''' Increase [[Haste]]. The game limits the bonus from effects to 60%.
* '''Paladins and Windblades.''' These two classes get 15% Haste from their abilities. Thus 45% from gear is sufficient for them.
* '''Item quality.''' Use {{ItemLink|Sivakrux|text=Sivakruxes}} to make gear [[Item Quality|Blessed]]. Use {{ItemLink|Planar Stone|text=Planar Stones}} to make gear [[Item Quality|Ascended]].

Sivakruxes and Planar Stones drop in all four raids.

<div>
<div class="mp-patch-title">Planar March</div>
<div class="mp-patch-body">Patch 0.7 added Erenshor's first [[Raids|raids]]: four Ethereal Planes entered through portals in the [[Reliquary Hall]], fought by three parties of five. Getting in takes a long chain through the level 30+ dungeons, and gearing fifteen characters is its own discipline.

Start with the '''[[Raids]]''' hub, then the '''[[Raid Unlocking Walkthrough]]'''. For running one: '''[[Class Roles in the Planar March Raid|Class Roles]]''' and '''[[Distributing Gear in the Planar March Raid|Distributing Gear]]'''.</div>

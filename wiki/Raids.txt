'''Raids''' are [[Erenshor]]'s endgame group content, introduced by the ''Planar March'' update (patch 0.7). Instead of the usual party of five, a raid fields three parties of five: fifteen characters, made up of the player and fourteen [[Simulated Players]] drawn from the player's [[Guild]].

Four raids are currently available. All four are Ethereal Planes reached through portals in the [[Reliquary Hall]], and each portal is opened by placing the matching rune in the Reliquary's outermost customization slots. Raid enemies are level 40–42 and raid bosses are level 43, so a raid roster should be at the level cap and in end-game [[Recommended Gear|pre-raid gear]] before starting.

Each raid's full enemy and boss roster is listed on its own zone page. [[Vitheo's Plane of Valor]] is unusual: most of its content is an eight-round arena gauntlet, and the loot for each round drops from a [[Vithean Chest|Vithean Chest]] rather than from the boss itself.

==Unlocking the Raids==

Raid access is gated behind a long chain that runs through most of Erenshor's level 30+ dungeons. Each of the four raid runes is made by combining an Inert Rune with an {{ItemLink|Eldrich Crystal}} at that raid's energy. In outline, the player must:

# '''Collect the four Inert Runes.''' Two are boss drops: {{ItemLink|Inert Rune of Vitheo}} from [[Apparition of Vitheo]] in [[Vitheo's Rest]], and {{ItemLink|Inert Rune of Fernalla}} from [[Fallen Fernalla]] in [[Blooming Sepulcher]]. Two are quest turn-ins: hand the {{ItemLink|Heart of Astra}} to the [[Freed Spirit of Raval]] for {{QuestLink|Raval's Last Wish}}, and hand both Braxonian portal shards to [[Braxon Elden]] for {{QuestLink|A Lost Portal}}.
# '''Open [[Secluded Sanctuary]].''' Complete {{QuestLink|Completing the Ritual}} by giving {{ItemLink|Loss Stone}}, {{ItemLink|Demented Brain}}, {{ItemLink|A Remnant of Sivakaya}}, and {{ItemLink|Nightmare Crystal}} to [[Lucian Revald]] on [[Stowaway's Step]].
# '''Farm four {{ItemLink|Eldrich Crystal|text=Eldrich Crystals}}.''' The [[Azynthian Keeper]] in [[Secluded Sanctuary]] always drops one.
# '''Power each rune.''' Give an Inert Rune and an {{ItemLink|Eldrich Crystal}} to the matching energy. Three are named [[Verdant Energy]], in [[Fernalla's Revival Plains]], [[Soluna's Landing]], and [[Vitheo's Rest]]. The fourth is the [[Elemental Energy]] in [[Fallen Braxonia]].
# '''Enable the portals.''' Place the four runes in the [[Reliquary Hall]] rune slots and press the build button.

For the complete step-by-step process, including every prerequisite dungeon and boss, see '''[[Raid Unlocking Walkthrough]]'''.

==Forming a Raid==

Raids are started from the [[Guild]] window using the '''Call Raid''' button. Several conditions apply:

* '''You must be in the [[Reliquary Hall]].''' Elsewhere the game replies ''"Raids must be formed in a Reliquary."'' A game option lifts this restriction.
* '''You must not be in a group.''' Dismiss any group members first, or the game replies ''"Dismiss your group before forming a raid."''
* '''Erenshor's Young cannot raid.''' Members of that guild are refused with ''"Erenshor's Young cannot participate in raids."''

Calling the raid summons your guild to the hall and automatically fills the roster with your A-Team [[Simulated Players]]. Additional Sims can be added by targeting them and adding them to a slot.

Only the [[Reliquary Hall]] and the four planes are raid-capable zones. Zoning into anything else breaks the raid up: ''"A strange force pulls your companions in different directions... (This is not a raidable zone)."''

While a raid is active, opponents below level 40 award no experience, so a standing raid cannot be used to farm ordinary content.

==The Raid Window==

The raid window replaces the group window. It holds fourteen [[Simulated Players|Sim Player]] slots plus a card for the player, who always sits in the first group. Its per-group controls are the core of raid leading:

* '''Group task''' — each of the three groups is independently set to assist the main group, assist the second group, assist the third group, focus that group's assigned target, or focus all adds.
* '''Main Assist / Main Tank''' — a single combined role, one member per group. The rest of the group attacks that member's target, and healers treat it as their first priority.
* '''Puller''' — one member per group. '''CC''' and '''Heals''' are ordinary toggles, so any number of members can carry them.
* '''Heal assignments''' — for each healer, a percentage of maximum health per target. The healer acts once that target drops below it, and zero disables the assignment entirely.
* '''DPS throttle''' — adds delay between attacks and spells, per group or raid-wide. At high settings Sims stop using spells and skills altogether.
* '''Loot dist.''' — awards an item to a raid member. Recipients are filtered to classes that can use the item, with {{ItemLink|Sivakrux|text=Sivakruxes}} and {{ItemLink|Planar Stone|text=Planar Stones}} special-cased to offer everyone.

Experience earned in a raid is shared with every raider, and each kill slightly improves your Sims' opinion of you.

==Preparing for a Raid==

Two dedicated guides cover raid preparation in depth:

* '''[[Class Roles in the Planar March Raid]]''' — what each class and build contributes, which debuffs stack, and how many tanks, healers, and damage dealers to bring.
* '''[[Distributing Gear in the Planar March Raid]]''' — stat priorities, weapon choices per build, aura selection, and which gear to upgrade first.

The short version, all of it guidance rather than hard requirement: tanks want Agility and AC, everyone wants their lowest resist raised as progression demands (the gear guide targets 140% before the late encounters and 170–190% by the end), and damage dealers want [[Haste]] at the game's 60% cap — though Paladins and Windblades need only 45% from gear, since their abilities supply the remaining 15%. Gear is upgraded to [[Item Quality|Blessed]] with {{ItemLink|Sivakrux|text=Sivakruxes}} and to [[Item Quality|Ascended]] with {{ItemLink|Planar Stone|text=Planar Stones}}, both of which drop throughout the raids.

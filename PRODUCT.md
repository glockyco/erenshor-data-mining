# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are the maintainer and trusted contributors who extract Erenshor data, review generated output, edit the wiki, operate deployments, and develop companion tools. They work in dense technical workflows where correctness, traceability, and efficient keyboard use matter more than visual novelty.

Players are the downstream users. They consult the wiki and interactive map or use companion mods while learning the game, planning progression, and navigating the world. These surfaces should make authoritative game data understandable without obscuring uncertainty or provenance.

## Product Purpose

Erenshor turns shipped game data into trustworthy public references and focused companion experiences. It joins extraction, validation, publishing, maps, and mods in one maintainable system. Success means maintainers can make safe, reviewable updates and players can find accurate answers quickly.

## Brand Personality

Authentic, precise, restrained.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS dashboard or layer decorative fantasy chrome over workflow tools. Avoid glass effects, ornamental game frames, novelty controls, and visual spectacle that competes with data. Prefer the established vocabulary of MediaWiki, the host platform, or the game surface instead of inventing a parallel interface language.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before decoration.** Make source data, identity, state, and failure visible. Visual treatment must clarify those facts.
2. **Use the host platform well.** Preserve familiar MediaWiki, web, desktop, and in-game interaction patterns so users can stay focused on their task.
3. **Earn game authenticity.** Echo Erenshor where game data or an in-game companion benefits from recognition. Keep maintenance and editing controls restrained.
4. **Make safe paths obvious.** Distinguish preview from mutation, fail before destructive actions, and leave content untouched when an operation cannot complete reliably.
5. **Support dense expert work without sacrificing legibility.** Use concise labels, clear hierarchy, deterministic ordering, and complete keyboard interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for web and wiki interfaces. Provide keyboard-complete workflows, visible focus, color-independent state communication, sufficient contrast, semantic labels and relationships, and reduced-motion behavior. Preserve usable fallbacks when JavaScript, pointer hover, or enhanced editors are unavailable.
